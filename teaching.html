ME 360 Signal Processing

A first course in signals and systems. On the signals side: Fourier analysis of analog and discrete-time signals, moving between time and frequency domains, sampling and aliasing. On the systems side: Laplace and z-transforms, impulse response and convolution. The ideas are applied to filter design, feedback control and simple machine learning.

  • Fourier series & transforms
  • Sampling & aliasing
  • Laplace & z-transforms
  • Convolution
  • Filter design
  • PI control
  • Perceptron & linear regression
Prerequisite
ME 340, Dynamics of Mechanical Systems
Credit
3.5 hours, lecture plus lab
Texts
Course notes; Ambardar, Analog and Digital Signal Processing, 2nd ed.

TAM 541 Mathematical Methods I

The mathematics behind engineering analysis. Vector and tensor algebra and complex-variable methods; ordinary differential equations, including existence and uniqueness; analytic and numerical solution methods, power series and special functions; eigenvalue problems, Green’s functions, Laplace transforms and stability of solutions, with applications drawn from mechanics.

  • Complex analysis
  • Residue theorem
  • Vector & inner product spaces
  • Linear operators
  • Eigenproblems
  • ODEs & linear systems
Prerequisites
MATH 241 or MATH 380; and one of MATH 385, MATH 386 or MATH 441
Supplementary text
Greenberg, Advanced Engineering Mathematics

TAM 542 Mathematical Methods II

The continuation of TAM 541. Modeling and inequalities, elements of functional analysis; partial differential equations, existence and uniqueness, second-order equations and hyperbolic conservation laws; numerical methods, eigenfunction expansions, integral transforms and fundamental solutions, with applications drawn from mechanics.

  • Functional analysis
  • PDEs
  • Conservation laws
  • Eigenfunction expansions
  • Integral transforms
  • Fundamental solutions
Prerequisite
TAM 541
Supplementary text
Greenberg, Advanced Engineering Mathematics

ECE 515 Control System Theory & Design

Feedback control systems from the state-space point of view: basic principles, modeling, analysis, stability, structural properties such as controllability and observability, optimization, and design to meet specifications.

  • Linear algebra for control
  • Solution of linear ODEs
  • Lyapunov stability
  • Controllability & observability
  • Realization theory
  • Observer design
  • Optimal control
Prerequisite
ECE 486, or an equivalent first course in control
Credit
4 hours
Cross-listed
ME 540, SE 522
Reference
Başar, Meyn & Perkins, Lecture Notes on Control System Theory and Design (free on arXiv)

ECE 528 Analysis of Nonlinear Systems

Nonlinear dynamic systems: the phase plane and phenomena with no linear counterpart, stability, invariance theory, asymptotic methods and feedback design.

  • Phase plane analysis
  • Limit cycles
  • Lyapunov stability
  • LaSalle invariance
  • Perturbation & averaging
  • Nonlinear feedback design
Prerequisites
ECE 515; and MATH 444 or MATH 447
Credit
4 hours
