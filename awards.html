searchers consensus ★ highest peak

No map, no gradient: they find the top by agreeing.

The banner is a swarm searching a landscape it cannot see for its highest peak, using consensus-based optimization: each searcher is pulled toward a consensus that favors whoever stands highest. Climbing to the top together, students and advisor, is how most of the honors below were earned.
