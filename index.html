Research focus

Our group works on Probabilistic problems in Estimation, Dynamics and Controls (P.E.D.C.). We are interested in the theory and algorithms for nonlinear estimation, stochastic control, and reinforcement learning.

We are known for inventing the feedback particle filter (FPF), an algorithm for nonlinear estimation that was featured in the August 2021 issue of IEEE Control Systems Magazine. We have a knack for problems that were long believed to be unsolvable. A recent example is our work on duality for nonlinear filtering, which earned Jin Won Kim the Best Student Paper Award at IEEE CDC 2019.

The work also leaves the lab. Former student Adam Tilton won the university-wide Cozad entrepreneurship competition in 2014 and co-founded the motion-sensing startup Rithmio, whose technology portfolio was acquired by Bosch Sensortec in 2017.
